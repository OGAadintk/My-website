Epic Stories

The great Sanskrit epics and related traditions have influenced art, literature, performance and culture for centuries.

Ramayana

A major epic centered on Rama, Sita, Lakshmana and Hanuman, exploring duty, loyalty and devotion.

Mahabharata

A vast epic of family, conflict, ethics and dharma, containing the Bhagavad Gita.

Bhagavad Gita

A dialogue between Krishna and Arjuna addressing action, duty, knowledge and devotion.

Stories of Hanuman

Hanuman is celebrated for devotion, courage, strength and service, especially in the Ramayana tradition.
