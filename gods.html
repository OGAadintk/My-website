Gods & Deities

Hindu traditions include a vast and diverse range of deities and forms of the divine.

𑁍

Brahma

Associated with creation and knowledge in the Trimurti.

✦

Vishnu

Associated with preservation and celebrated through many avatars.

ॐ

Shiva

Associated with transformation, meditation and the cosmic cycle.

☀

Surya

The sun deity, honored in many Hindu practices and traditions.

🌺

Devi

The divine feminine appears in many forms including Durga, Lakshmi and Saraswati.

🐘

Ganesha

Widely honored as a remover of obstacles and associated with wisdom.
