Wisdom

Timeless Ideas

Different Hindu schools and traditions approach these ideas in diverse ways.

Dharma

A broad concept involving duty, conduct, order and living appropriately within one's circumstances.

Karma

Often understood as the relationship between intentional action and its consequences.

Bhakti

Devotion and loving dedication toward a chosen deity or form of the divine.

Yoga

A broad family of spiritual and philosophical disciplines aimed at transformation and realization.

Atman

A concept concerning the self, interpreted differently across Hindu philosophical schools.

Moksha

Liberation from the cycle of rebirth, a central goal in several Hindu traditions.

“You have a right to perform your prescribed duties, but you are not entitled to the fruits of your actions.”Bhagavad Gita 2.47 — one common English translation
